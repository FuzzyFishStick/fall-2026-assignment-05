Our story

Welcome to the Lodge.

A warm light in a world where the sun never rises.

The Moonlit Lodge

A home for travelers beneath the endless night.

Somewhere beyond the mountains lies a town that has never seen the sunrise. No one remembers exactly when the daylight disappeared. Some say the town was cursed. Others whisper that an ancient wizard stole the sun. A few believe the moon itself is protecting everyone from something waiting beyond the darkness.

At the edge of that town stands The Moonlit Lodge. Part marketplace, part adventurer's rest, and part gathering place, the Lodge has welcomed travelers for generations.

Whether you're a seasoned adventurer preparing for a dangerous expedition or simply someone looking for a warm meal and a good story, there is always a place for you here.

A note from the keeper
